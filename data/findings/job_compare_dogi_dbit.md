# DOGI vs DBIT Compare

**Date:** 2026-08-31
**Status:** Unproven
**Verdict:** Cannot verify via public APIs alone

## Summary

Compared DOGI (claimed 21M supply) and DBIT (claimed 2.1T supply) as separate tickers across public indexers.

## Methodology

1. Queried public indexers for both DOGI and DBIT inscription data
2. Compared responses to determine if both tickers are recognized

## Findings

- Public APIs cannot fetch inscription content for either ticker
- Most indexers return 404 or require authentication
- No differentiation between the two tickers could be established

## Conclusion

Cannot verify whether DOGI and DBIT are treated as separate tickers by public indexers.
