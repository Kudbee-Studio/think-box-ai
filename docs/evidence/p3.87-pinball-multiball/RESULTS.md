# P3.87 Pinball v0.6 in the dashboard: upper deck, ball traps, a lock you can reach, multiball

The dashboard's `/pinball/` copy is refreshed from the standalone project (`~/projects/pinball`, commit 6bb371b, v0.6) after #426 brought it to v0.3. It now has: two more bumpers, a bumper-bank jackpot, super combos, an upper deck (a second layer) reached by a trap at the top, and a green LOCK at the bottom of a funnel on the left wall that holds a ball for 3 seconds and then shoots it out with up to two free extra balls (multiball, at most 3 balls at once). The main-table collision geometry (the ball-sticking fix) is unchanged since v0.2.1.

## What went wrong on the way (and why this is v0.6, not v0.5)
The v0.5 lock worked when a test placed a ball on it, but the owner reported "there are no multiball". Measuring showed why: in 150 simulated balls (about 5,300 s of play) it was triggered 7 times, about once in 13 minutes, because a ball had to roll slowly over a small spot on the wall. A first fix (funnel, bigger labelled lock, higher capture speed) made it reachable in simulation, but real-browser runs then locked only 1 time in 5. The cause was a second, deeper problem: each frame was cut into differently sized physics pieces, which changes how much energy a ball sliding along a wall loses, so with uneven frame times a full plunger pull locked 24 of 60 times instead of 60 of 60. The physics now advances in fixed 1/480 s pieces with the remainder carried over. A PR for v0.5 (#427's first head) passed its gates but was not merged because it carried the unreachable lock.
