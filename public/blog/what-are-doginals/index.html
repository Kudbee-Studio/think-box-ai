What Are Doginals? The Complete Guide

Published: 2026-09-01 | Reading time: 5 min

Introduction

Doginals are digital inscriptions on the Dogecoin blockchain. Think of them like NFTs, but better — the data is baked directly onto the blockchain, not stored on third-party servers. This makes them truly permanent and censorship-resistant.

How Doginals Work

Each Doginal is created by inscribing data (images, text, or any file) onto a Dogecoin transaction. The inscription follows a specific protocol:

"ord"
OP_1
"text/plain; charset=utf8"
OP_0
"Woof!"

This data lives on the Dogecoin blockchain forever. No IPFS, no Arweave, no third-party hosting. Just pure on-chain data.

Why Doginals Matter

  • True ownership — Your inscription is on-chain, not on someone else's server
  • Censorship-resistant — No one can take it down or alter it
  • Permanent — As long as Dogecoin exists, your inscription exists
  • Self-custody — Hold it in your own wallet, no middlemen

How to Buy Doginals

  3. Browse collections on Think Box AI

Popular Collections

The most popular Doginal collections include Doginal Dogs (10,000 pixel dogs), Doge Maps (virtual real estate), and DRC-20 tokens (fungible tokens on Dogecoin).

DRC-20 Tokens

DRC-20 is a token standard for Dogecoin. Unlike inscriptions (which are unique), DRC-20 tokens are fungible — like ERC-20 on Ethereum. Popular DRC-20 tokens include DOGI, DBIT, and DHUB.

Security Tips

Always verify the inscription ID before buying. Check the provenance history. And never share your seed phrase with anyone.
