How Think Box Works

Intent → Hat → Box → Tools → Artifacts → Verdict

The North-Star Loop

Think Box AI runs narrow research jobs against live public APIs, persists findings to an immutable proof file, and delivers a verdict. Every step is transparent and auditable.

Intent

Define the research question. What claim are you testing? What sources will you query?

Researcher Hat

The first hat. Queries live public indexers, compares responses, records disagreement.

Proof File

Every job produces a human-readable finding with raw evidence. No marketing spin.

Verdict

Succeeded, failed, unproven, or blocked. Never overstated. Never hidden.
